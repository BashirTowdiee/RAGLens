# Refund Policy

## Purpose

This policy explains how RAGLens Corp support teams assess refund requests.

## Standard refunds

Customers may request a refund within 14 days of purchase when the product has not been used beyond the trial threshold. The trial threshold is defined as three completed exports or 60 minutes of processed audio, whichever happens first.

## Service outages

When a confirmed service outage prevents core product usage for more than four hours in a billing month, support may issue a partial account credit. Refunds for outage impact require approval from support operations.

## Non-refundable cases

Refunds are not available for accidental deletion of customer-created content, unsupported third-party integrations, or usage that exceeds the trial threshold.

## Escalation

Refund requests above 500 AUD require finance review. Refund disputes involving privacy, legal, or regulatory concerns must be escalated to the legal operations queue.
