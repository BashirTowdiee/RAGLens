# Remote Work Policy v2

## Purpose

This policy supersedes Remote Work Policy v1 and defines the current remote work rules for RAGLens Corp employees.

## Eligibility

Permanent employees may work remotely up to three days per week after completing probation. Contractors may work remotely up to one day per week when their statement of work allows it and their manager approves it.

## Office attendance

Engineering, product, and support teams must attend the office on Tuesdays and Thursdays. The Thursday attendance rule applies from 1 July 2026.

## Equipment

Employees may request a company laptop, monitor, keyboard, mouse, and headset. Home internet costs may be reimbursed up to 60 AUD per month when approved by a manager.

## Exceptions

Temporary remote work exceptions can be approved by a department head for medical, caregiving, severe weather, or public transport disruption reasons.

## Review cycle

The people operations team reviews this policy every six months and records approved changes in the policy changelog.
