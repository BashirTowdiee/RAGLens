# Remote Work Policy v1

## Purpose

This policy defines the first version of RAGLens Corp's remote work rules for permanent employees.

## Eligibility

Permanent employees may work remotely up to two days per week after completing their probation period. Contractors require manager approval before working remotely.

## Office attendance

Engineering, product, and support teams must attend the office on Tuesdays. Team leads may nominate an additional collaboration day during major planning cycles.

## Equipment

Employees may request a company laptop, monitor, keyboard, and mouse. Home internet costs are not reimbursed under version 1 of this policy.

## Exceptions

Temporary remote work exceptions can be approved by a department head for medical, caregiving, or travel disruption reasons.
