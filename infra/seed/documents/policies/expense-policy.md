# Expense Policy

## Purpose

This policy explains which business expenses RAGLens Corp employees may claim and how approvals work.

## Standard approvals

Expenses under 250 AUD require line manager approval. Expenses from 250 AUD to 1,000 AUD require department head approval. Expenses above 1,000 AUD require finance approval before purchase.

## Travel

Domestic flights must be economy class unless an executive approves an exception. Accommodation should be booked through the company travel portal when available.

## Meals

Meal claims are capped at 35 AUD for breakfast, 45 AUD for lunch, and 75 AUD for dinner. Alcohol is not reimbursable unless it is part of an approved client event.

## Remote work equipment

Remote work equipment must follow the current Remote Work Policy. Internet reimbursement is handled under the remote work process, not the general expense process.
