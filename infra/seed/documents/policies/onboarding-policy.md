# Onboarding Policy

## Purpose

This policy describes the onboarding process for new RAGLens Corp employees.

## Pre-start setup

People operations creates the employee profile at least five business days before the start date. IT must provision email, identity access, laptop, and required group memberships before day one.

## First week

New employees complete security training, privacy training, payroll setup, and product orientation during the first week. Engineering hires also complete repository access setup and local development environment setup.

## Buddy programme

Every new employee is assigned a buddy for their first 30 days. The buddy answers process questions and helps the employee understand team norms.

## Probation checkpoint

Managers complete probation checkpoints at 30, 60, and 90 days. Remote work eligibility starts only after probation has been completed.
