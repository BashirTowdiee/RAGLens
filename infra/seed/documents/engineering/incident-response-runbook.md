# Incident Response Runbook

## Purpose

This runbook defines how RAGLens Corp handles production incidents.

## Severity levels

Severity 1 incidents cause a complete production outage, data exposure, or customer funds risk. Severity 2 incidents cause major feature degradation for many users. Severity 3 incidents cause limited user impact or internal operational disruption.

## Roles

Every Severity 1 or Severity 2 incident must have an incident commander, communications lead, investigation lead, and scribe. One person may not hold both incident commander and communications lead roles during a Severity 1 incident.

## Response targets

Severity 1 incidents require acknowledgement within five minutes and an external status update within 20 minutes. Severity 2 incidents require acknowledgement within 15 minutes and an external status update within 60 minutes when customers are affected.

## Post-incident review

Severity 1 and Severity 2 incidents require a post-incident review within five business days. Action items must have an owner, due date, and severity tag.
